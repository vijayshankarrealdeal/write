Frequently asked

Questions & honest answers.

Is InkSpaceX really free?
Yes — the entire writing and reading experience is free, forever, with no ads. Pro is an optional upgrade for writers who want extras like an AI co-writer, audio chapters, and custom domains. We're funded by Pro subscribers, not by selling your data.
How does InkSpaceX compare to Scrivener?
InkSpaceX is a modern Scrivener alternative that runs on the web, iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows. Scrivener is desktop-only on macOS and Windows (plus a separate iOS app) and uses Dropbox for sync. Both offer multi-section editing — InkSpaceX calls it Scrivenings mode — footnotes, snapshots, and goal tracking. The shorter version: same writing depth, every device, free. See the full side-by-side comparison.
Can I read or write offline?
Yes. Every device keeps a local copy of the stories you've opened and the drafts you're working on. Edits sync automatically the next time you connect, with conflict-free merges across devices — so a flight, a tunnel, or a power cut never costs you a sentence.
Can I import my work from Scrivener or Word?
Yes. You can import .docx files directly, and use Scrivener's Compile-to-.docx step to migrate an existing project — chapters become sections in InkSpaceX, formatting and footnotes are preserved. A native .scriv project importer is on the roadmap; drop us a note and we'll prioritise it.
Does InkSpaceX have AI?
Yes — InkSpaceX Pro ships an opt-in AI co-writer that helps you grow, shrink, rewrite, or unstick a passage without leaving the editor. The editor itself works perfectly without it. Your drafts are never used to train any AI model — ours, or anyone else's — and the AI runs against isolated providers under strict no-retention contracts. See how it compares to Sudowrite.
Where do my drafts and stories live?
Your drafts sync between devices via our servers (encrypted in transit and at rest), and we keep an offline copy on every device you sign in to. You can export everything as Markdown or PDF at any time, or delete your account in two clicks.
Do you train AI on my writing?
Never. Your drafts and published work are not used to train any AI model — ours, or anyone else's. The Pro AI co-writer runs against isolated providers under strict no-retention contracts.
Which devices does InkSpaceX work on?
Web (any modern browser), iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux — with full offline support on each. Same drafts, same bookmarks, same highlights, in perfect sync. See the 2026 buyer's guide for how that compares to other writing tools.
What about ads, tracking, or feed algorithms?
None of those things exist on InkSpaceX. There is no ad network, no third-party analytics, and no algorithmic feed. You see what you choose to follow, ordered by time. That's it.
