Hearing their words, Valmiki answered:

> “This lady is Sita, the consort of the virtuous Rama, she is the daughter-in-law of King Dasaratha, the chaste daughter of Janaka. Though innocent, her husband has renounced her, we should all therefore protect her; she is worthy of our affection! Such is my wish, who am your spiritual preceptor; at my behest, do ye pay her every honour.”

Having placed Sita in the charge of those female ascetics, the great and illustrious Sage Valmiki, followed by his disciples, returned to his hermitage.
