Then Sugriva, bowing low, said to Rama: "Hear me and I will tell thee all.

"When Bali pursued the giant Dundubhi, in the form of a buffalo, in the direction of the Malaya mountain, Mahisba 1 enteted a cave in that mountain and Bali, desirous of slaying that Asura, followed him.

"I remained obediently at the entrance of the cave, but a whole year passed and Bali did not re-emerge. Then the cavetn was filled with foaming blood which gushed forth, and seeing this, I was terrified and consumed with a burning grief on account of my brother. Distracted, I rellected: ‘My elder brother is certainly dead’ and I placed a rock, as large as a bill, at the mouth of the cave, thinking ‘The buffalo will not be able to come out and will die’; after which I returned to Kishkindha giving up all hope of Bali being alive.

"There obtaining the mighty kingdom with Tara and Ruma, surrounded by my friends, I began to pass my days in peace.

"That bull among monkeys, however, having slain Dundubhi returned, and trembling with fear, in all humility, I made over the crown to him.
